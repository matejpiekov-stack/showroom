Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Showroom collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and tools.

1. Information We Collect

  • Vehicle details you enter into our tools (make, model, year, mileage, condition, etc.)
  • Photos you upload for photo enhancement
  • Basic account information if you create an account (e.g. email address)
  • Payment information if you subscribe to a paid plan (processed by our payment provider, not stored by us directly)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate price estimates, descriptions, and enhanced photos using third-party AI services
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our tools
  • To communicate with you about your account or subscription

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party AI providers (such as Google's Gemini API) to process the information you submit in order to generate results. Data sent to these providers is subject to their own privacy and data-handling terms.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide our services, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@showroom.com.
